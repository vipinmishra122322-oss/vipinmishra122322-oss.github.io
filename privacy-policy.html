Introduction

Welcome to Count Your Cash – Cash Counter Pro ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application available on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is written in plain language so you understand exactly what happens with your data.

Simple answer: The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All your cash tally data is stored locally on your device only.
2

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect:

✗ Name or Email ✗ Phone Number ✗ Location Data ✗ Bank Details ✗ Account Information ✗ Biometric Data ✗ Contacts ✗ Camera Access

What is stored locally on your device:

  • Your cash denomination counts and totals (Notes & Coins entries)
  • Your saved tally history (dates and totals only)
  • Your preferred app theme (Dark / Light mode)
This data never leaves your device. It is stored using Android's secure local storage (AsyncStorage) and is not accessible to us or any third party.
3

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information to serve relevant ads. This is standard practice for free apps.

What Google AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID) – used to show relevant ads
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
Note: We have configured AdMob to request non-personalized ads by default (requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true), which limits the data Google uses for ad targeting.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4

App Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET — Required only for Google AdMob to load advertisements. Your cash data is never sent over the internet.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Required only when you choose to save your receipt image to your gallery. The app only saves the image you explicitly request to save.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used by AdMob to check if network is available before loading ads.
No other permissions are requested. We follow the principle of least privilege — only the minimum permissions needed for the app to function.
5

Data Security

Your tally data is stored using Android's local storage (AsyncStorage). To protect your data:

  • We have disabled Android backup (allowBackup: false), so your data cannot be extracted via ADB backup tools
  • No data is transmitted to any server — ever
  • We have no database, no backend, no cloud storage
  • HTTPS-only enforced for all network communication (ads only)
✓ Offline-first ✓ No server ✓ Backup disabled ✓ HTTPS enforced
6

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The App is a financial utility tool intended for general adult use.

Since we collect no personal information from any user, children's data is equally protected — nothing is collected from any age group.

7

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Clear your tally history anytime from within the app using "Clear All History"
  • Uninstalling the App automatically deletes all locally stored data
  • No data exists on any server — there is nothing for us to delete on our end
